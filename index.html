01

Three knobs, set at every call

A flow-matching VLA runs its whole VLM, takes every denoising step from noise and executes a fixed number of actions, whatever the scene. DéjàVLA sets three knobs per call from how closely the scene matches what the policy has already solved.

VLM layers (first 2 always run)
Context

How much of the VLM to run

Every call runs the first two VLM layers. When the stored successor of the last match is close enough, the call stops there: no further layers, no action expert.

Reflection

How many denoising steps, from where

A close match starts from the recalled action chunk, re-noised to τ = 0.8, and takes one step. Only an unfamiliar scene runs the full schedule from noise.

How many actions to execute

Free motion is predictable; contact needs fresh observations. DéjàVLA executes 1.5× more actions when the chunk holds no gripper change, and the calibrated horizon otherwise.

03

Components

Four parts, no training: a memory and its key, a similarity router, a horizon rule, and a calibration that sets their thresholds on held-out episodes.

entries grouped by instruction; top-1 match

Two-layer memory retrieval

The memory holds one entry per call of each successful episode: its key, action chunk, robot state and place in the episode. The key comes from the second VLM layer: image and instruction tokens are averaged, centered, projected to 512 values each by a fixed random matrix, and joined with the robot state. A call searches only the entries of its own instruction and keeps the top-1 cosine match.

Similarity-guided denoising

The match's score s picks the path. If the stored successor of the previous match scores at least θ2 (and within 0.01 of the best match), its chunk is reused, at most twice in a row. Otherwise a match above θ1 starts one denoising step from its chunk re-noised to τ = 0.8. Below θ1, with no entry for the instruction, or when the last 8 scores drift beyond 3 standard deviations, the call runs the full schedule. For π0, whose actions are relative to the robot state, a recalled chunk is shifted to the current state.

Momentum: adaptive execution horizon

The call executes next = 1.5 × nanc actions when those actions hold no gripper open/close change, and nanc otherwise. π0 runs 16 or 24 actions and GR00T N1.7 8 or 12. π0.5's calibration found no longer horizon, and SmolVLA's extension lost success at calibration (69% against 75%), so both keep 10.

Online memory update and offline calibration

The memory starts from the successful calibration episodes. θ1 and θ2 are the 5th and 80th percentiles of top-1 scores on held-out episodes (SmolVLA: θ1 at the 1st), and the horizon extension is kept only if success stays within 3 points. During deployment, every successful episode joins the memory, which keeps at most 10,000 entries per suite and evicts the least recently used.
